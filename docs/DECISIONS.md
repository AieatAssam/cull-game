- 2026-10-12: Use the square gate and 45° rotation snaps, keep occlusion out of simulation, retain HOLD, and use film-set comedy; these follow the design draft defaults.
- 2026-10-12: The M1 projection uses a deterministic ground-plane orthographic transform at 55°; entity bounds use an equivalent conservative projected extent calculation, and renderer camera uses the matching yaw, pitch, and gate width.
- 2026-10-12: M2 starts with a deterministic Backlot strip and seed-driven entity spawns; the browser smoke test captures 390×844 and 1280×800 gate layouts.
- 2026-10-12: Add deterministic branch choices at path indices 4, 14, 24, 34, 44, 54 and 64; choose the available branch with the best heading dot product toward the gate centre, using the first option on ties.
- 2026-10-12: Use the Three.js renderer's actual CSS canvas size for the square gate; Playwright `frozen=1` snapshots manually step seeded input for repeatable checks.
- 2026-10-12: Keep the M2 human test as a sign-off gate; the 20-seed smart bot comparison missed its 1.3× target after one tuning pass.
- 2026-10-12: Continue into M3 per Assam's direction despite the M2 bot kill criterion; retain the failed ratio and pending human test as open evidence.
- 2026-10-12: Store hysteretic LOD tiers in simulation state and hashes; render Urchins with one instanced bucket per visible tier and omit sub-pixel instances. Debug tier previews may exceed gameplay zoom to capture all five tiers.
- 2026-10-12: Represent Extras as seeded member offsets with independent culling and 0.8 u/s motion; hold Swarm spawn points unconsumed below 4 lpx; model Bridge plank loss at wide tiers; collect or steal Reels through CAST cuts.

- 2026-10-12: Keep M6 Shoot progression opt-in with `?shoot=1` until its full 20-seed duration gate passes; the same entry point exposes all four ordered segments for scripted validation.
- 2026-10-12: Use 200-seed M5 results as the current acceptance record: Adaptive 88%, Tracker 100%, Adaptive/Tracker 0.936×, Wide/Adaptive 0.489×, Close/Adaptive 0.831×; Chaos 1,000/1,000 finite and generation p95 3.55 ms. Preserve all red gates in progress and keep CI reporting them non-blocking.

- 2026-10-12: Replace primitive cast visuals with a cohesive "Handmade Backlot" of transparent painted sprites, tactile fabric/clay materials, distinct palette identities, 8-way camera-relative facing, and authored identity-preserving LOD art; keep the 55° orthographic camera and simulation bounds. This user-directed visual change intentionally introduces local sprite atlases despite the original texture-free rendering plan.
- 2026-10-12: Cap pan at 0.3 gate-widths per second (about 3.6× the medium Talent speed at the default zoom) and lower acceleration to 0.9 gate-widths/s² so the camera eases in about a third of a second. The in-motion threshold drops to 0.12 gate-widths/s, because the old 0.6 threshold is now above full pan and would freeze the Stagehand. A presentation-only dead zone pulls the frame back when the Talent nears the edge; it is not part of the simulation, so bots and replays are unchanged by it.
- 2026-10-12: Generated scenes and Shoot scenes 0–2 carry two shot-list gates, included in the state hash. Defang completes once the zoom reaches 36 or a hazard is cut within 1.5 u (score +250). Portrait completes only after that, while zoom is between 16 and 34. The Talent waits, burning film at double rate, until the open gate is made. The finale has no gates, because AdaptiveBot holds zoom 48 there. This is what moves Tracker completion from 100% to 0% and Adaptive/Tracker distance from 0.936× to 5.025× on 200 seeds.
- 2026-10-12: Glass and bridges within 0.12 projected units past the gate are not cut, so a one-tick zoom twitch cannot delete the floor the Talent is about to walk. Boulders kill only at close-up tier; at medium they are a prop you can still cut. Both changes are why Adaptive completion reached 99% without letting a fixed zoom finish the strip.
- 2026-10-12: The three-scene Shoot is the default take. `?scene=1` keeps a single generated strip, `?roster=1` and `?validated=1` keep their fixtures, and `?shoot=1` still opens the Shoot. Daily seeds and replay hashes still come from the same sim.
- 2026-10-12: The play view uses the Dailies tabletop look in `docs/art/style-guide.md`: one palette, per-scene sets, a larger walked Talent drawn in code, Fraunces and Source Sans 3 (OFL, bundled), and no cast AABB debug lines. Cut feedback (slice, peel, stamp, shake, 70 ms hit-stop, synthesised splice) is presentation-only and does not enter the state hash.
- 2026-10-12: Bible M4 set dressing stays out of the simulation. The bible's 0.15 u path rise is about 3 px at zoom 30, so the felt ribbon is drawn at 0.72 u. The outline is filleted so a stair of cells reads as one hand-cut strip, with stitches, a frayed hem and a lighter lip on the key side. Each Shoot scene has its own quiet dressing. Cork, felt and paper are canvas grain, not stripes. Replay hashes and bot metrics do not move. The exposure rule waits for bible M10.
- 2026-10-13: Bible M2 (fix Stage 0 before new content). Rule changes, all in `src/sim`, each with tests in `tests/m2.test.ts`, `tests/sim.test.ts` and `tests/shoot.test.ts`:
  - `defang` loses its `zoom >= 36` branch. It now needs a close cut made after the Talent reached the gate (`ShotGoal.opened`).
  - Every defang gate gets a stand-in Urchin on its mark, `gateMarkDistance` 1.4 u ahead of the held Talent. That is inside the 1.5 u close-call radius and out of reach at any tier, so the gate is always solvable. The stand-in is exempt from pop-in exclusion. If it is framed out before its shot is called, it is unloaded intact rather than cut. It leaves once the shot is made.
  - Gate positions are seeded per scene (`defangGateFraction`, `portraitGateGap`). They slide forward off fork detours and away from Glass.
  - Glass framed out farther than `glassShatterRadius` (6 u) from the Talent is unloaded intact and respawns when framed again. Only nearer Glass, which was just on screen beside the Talent, shatters.
  - The hostile camera uses the same visibility test as every other cast member. It fails the take only after its tally has held the Talent in its cone, while on screen, for `hostileLockSeconds` (1 s). Framing it out or leaving its cone resets the tally (`lockTicks`). It cuts only spawned, on-screen cast, and never Glass or bridges.
  - A Wide crowd tramples on contact with a visible member (`extrasContactRadius`), not the 2 u disc around its centre.
  - The Wrap strike line is measured along the set path behind the Talent. The old linear x-to-length scale put it ahead of the Talent on paths that wind early, which deleted the floor under the Talent.
  - Generator: ≥ 50% of Urchins (`aheadEntryShare` 0.6, seeded) enter ahead on the walked path and roll down it (`pathBound`).
  - Not changed: hazard kinds, speeds, the film economy, and the portrait gate.

- 2026-10-13 (LOST HER): The out-of-frame rule is graded instead of a 1 s cliff. The Talent is "lost" when she is outside the gate and outside every friendly monitor cone. The same `offscreenTicks` counter drives all three stages:
  - The first `offscreenGrace` (0.5 s) costs nothing, so a whip-pan or a corner graze is not punished.
  - After that, film drains an extra `offscreenFilmDrainPerSecond` (4 film-seconds per second) on top of the normal burn.
  - The take fails `lost-talent` at `offscreenFailSeconds` (3 s). Reframing her resets the counter.
  - Why: the 1 s fail was binary and hard to read, so players died before they understood why. A longer window with a visible, continuous cost teaches the rule ("off-screen = gone") and leaves room to recover, and losing her is still never free. The burn uses the existing film resource, so it adds no new meter.
  - Presentation (render-only, not in the hash): a pulsing carmine gate edge, a "LOST HER" banner with a countdown bar to the cut, an edge arrow toward her, a red film meter with a "−4s/s" tag, and a lost toast and coach line from `describeShot`. Debug hook: `__cull.lostDemo(seconds)`.
  - Balance (`--stage=0 --mode=both --seeds=200 --jitter=8`): completion is unchanged for every bot. Adaptive is 100% on strip and Shoot. Tracker, Gatekeeper, Metronome, Wide, Close and Chaos are all 0%. Only Metronome and Chaos travel further before the cut (strip 20.5→24.2 and 21.8→28.3; Shoot 27.9→32.5 and 29.9→37.9). Passive bots never lose her, so the softer fail cannot help them coast. The film drain was not tuned.
  - Hashes: the scripted sweep in `tests/m2.test.ts` loses the Talent, so its strip-world and generated-strip hashes changed. Strip world: seed 1 `4500bdc4`, seed 19 `5f4a7114`, seed 2026 `ec251869`. Generated strip: `4339a9ff`, `af0d6135`, `85774155`. The fair-Adaptive Shoot hashes did not change (`6ebb4e8c`, `ce00d1c4`, `38027c06`) because Adaptive never stays out of frame past the grace.
- 2026-10-13 (fast zoom): Zoom was too slow to frame out the first Urchins. Players reported "you die instantly". Measured before: holding a zoom key (input 60) took 7.07 s from widest (48) to tightest (10), and full input (slider/pinch/buttons) took 3.35 s.
  - Sim: the zoom speed is now a response curve over the same −127…127 input, `zoomLogRate(u) = ln(zoomRate) · u · (1 + zoomBoost · u⁴)`, with `zoomRate` 1.6 and `zoomBoost` 8.6. Light inputs keep almost the old speed, so the bots' 32/64 trims and the replay format are unchanged. Full input is ≈ ×90/s, so widest→tightest at full input takes 0.35 s.
  - Controls (`src/ui/zoom-control.ts`): a tap on −/+ (also Z/X/=, the wheel, and the on-screen − + buttons) snaps to the next framing step in `zoomSteps` (10 / 16 / 24 / 38 / 48). The snap is eased by a proportional input and lands in ≈ 0.12–0.18 s with no overshoot. Holding past 0.15 s ramps to full input, so widest→tightest held takes 0.48 s. Sequential taps take 0.62 s, and a burst of taps takes 0.42 s. Pinch and the slider set a continuous target that is eased the same way. The old bang-bang ±127 drive would oscillate at the new speed.
  - Bots: no change. They use the same input channel, and their light inputs are barely affected. Balance (`--stage=0 --mode=both --seeds=200 --jitter=8`): strip Adaptive 100%, Tracker/Gatekeeper/Metronome/Wide/Close/Chaos 0%. Shoot Adaptive 99.5% (1 film death), all others 0%. Jitter: Gatekeeper 0% of variants, Adaptive 100%. No compensating pressure was needed.
  - Hashes: the fair-Adaptive Shoot hashes changed, to seed 1 `cbfefb6f`, seed 19 `46fc3197`, seed 2026 `9951f3d0`. Adaptive's ±64 zoom is now slightly faster. The strip-world and generated-strip hashes are unchanged, because the scripted sweep only uses zoom input 1.
- 2026-10-13 (loop fixes, from `docs/design/loop-review-2026-10-13.md`): human-like bots cleared 0% of Backlot. The browser leash made the defang cut geometrically unreachable.
  - Input (`src/ui/input-map.ts`, render-only, not in the hash): the leash is gone. `assistInput` is a soft lost-her assist. It does nothing while she is in frame. Once she is out, it blends toward her, ramping over the `offscreenGrace` to at most 30%. It is off while the player pans near an open defang gate or a hazard within `closeCallRadius` + 1 of her. A close Urchin can now be panned out of frame right next to her.
  - Touch (render-only): a one-finger drag is 1:1. The world under the finger follows it (`dragCameraTarget` turns px into a world displacement at the current zoom, yaw and aspect). `panToward` drives the camera onto that target and brakes, so it stops with no drift. Device parity: a drag pans no faster than held keys (`KEY_PAN`). This was tuning try 2: at full 127 input, touch cleared Backlot 91.5% against keys' 69.5%.
  - Sim (`unloadsIntact` and `markShots`): a gate stand-in framed out far before its shot is called is now **cut** (CUT stamp, logged in `cuts`, no close-call credit) instead of unloading intact and silently coming back. When the Talent reaches the mark, a cut stand-in is re-dressed there, because defang still needs a close cut made since the gate opened. Far Glass still unloads intact.
  - HUD: a far hazard cut stamps "TOO FAR" and says "Cut — but too far from her…". A re-dressed stand-in says "Shot called: the stand-in is back on its mark. Cut it close to her." The defang toast and third coach line now explain this rule.
  - Hashes: fair-Adaptive Shoot seed 1 `cbfefb6f` → `e0c89e0d`, seed 19 `46fc3197` → `a80d24ad`. Seed 2026 is unchanged (`9951f3d0`), as are the strip-world and generated-strip hashes. Adaptive frames out some stand-ins early on those two seeds; they are now cut and re-dressed rather than unloaded.
  - Balance (`--stage=0 --mode=both --seeds=200 --jitter=8`), Backlot clear: human-keys 69.5%, human-touch 65.5%. Adaptive Shoot 99.5%. All passive and scripted bots 0%.
